What is Escape from Tarkov?

Escape from Tarkov is a hardcore, realistic online first-person action RPG and extraction shooter developed by Battlestate Games

Set in a fictional, war-torn Russian industrial city, players enter matches ("raids") to scavenge for gear,

fight other players and AI, and attempt to escape alive.
